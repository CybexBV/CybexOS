# Cybex OS 1.0.39

Wiz now appears and vanishes in a puff of smoke. Showing him makes him pop up out of a little cloud at his place, with a few sparks of his magic, and hiding him makes him shrink away behind one — poof. The old flight in from the corner of the screen is gone, and he appears exactly where you left him, without sliding there first. With reduced motion he appears and goes at once, as before.

Reading update history and build logs at the computer no longer asks for the administrator password. Wiz uses them when he researches an update problem, and each command asked again — a short log even twice. Looking is now free in an active session at the machine; every change still asks.

The assistant's window also stops drawing in the background when nothing of it is on screen, which saves a little power.

This is a source update with no new ISO. Installation, boot, physical hardware and staged-updater qualification are separate from source tests and package builds.
